## 6. APP STORE DESCRIPTION

Your time is too valuable for charging stations.

Chercharge is an electric vehicle charging concierge service designed to make EV charging more convenient.

Through the Chercharge app, eligible customers can manage their Chercharge account, add eligible vehicles, review available services, request or schedule eligible Chercharge services, receive service updates, manage qualifying promotional benefits, and review applicable booking information.

When Chercharge service is available for an eligible booking, the Chercharge concierge experience may include coordinating the pickup of an eligible electric vehicle, transportation of the vehicle for charging, charging coordination, and return of the vehicle to an approved location.

### CHERCHARGE FEATURES

- Create and manage your Chercharge account.
- Add and manage eligible electric vehicles.
- Review available Chercharge services.
- Request or schedule eligible service.
- Select eligible pickup and return information.
- Review service pricing before purchase.
- Receive booking and service status updates.
- Manage qualifying Founding Customer promotional benefits.
- Upload or capture vehicle documentation when required for vehicle condition or service support.
- Review applicable service history and account information.

### FOUNDING CUSTOMER OFFERS

Eligible pre-launch customers may have access to limited Chercharge Founding Customer promotional rates.

The limited $10 Founding Vehicle Rate is available to a maximum of five accepted qualifying customers and applies only to one specifically enrolled eligible vehicle per accepted customer.

The $10 Founding Vehicle Rate is a per-eligible-service promotional base rate. It is not unlimited service for a single $10 payment.

The rate does not transfer to a replacement, substitute, additional, rental, loaner, family, business, or newly purchased vehicle.

When the original enrolled Founding Vehicle is permanently no longer in service, the applicable $10 Founding Vehicle Rate ends.

Eligible customers may also have access to a $39.99 per-eligible-service promotional rate for the applicable one-year promotional period.

Promotional restrictions, service eligibility, vehicle requirements, service area limitations, charging costs, third-party charges, and other applicable terms are disclosed before the applicable purchase and are governed by the applicable Chercharge agreement.

### PRE-LAUNCH NOTICE

Chercharge may be in a pre-launch phase in some or all intended service areas.

Purchasing or reserving an eligible pre-launch promotional benefit does not guarantee immediate service, a specific public launch date, a specific appointment time, or service in every geographic area.

Applicable refund rights are described in the Chercharge Pre-Launch Founding Customer Promotional Rate Agreement.

### SERVICE AVAILABILITY

Chercharge service is subject to eligible service areas, driver availability, vehicle eligibility, safety requirements, charging availability, operating capacity, and applicable booking terms.

Chercharge does not guarantee that every service request will be accepted.

Chercharge is not an emergency response service, towing company, electric utility, vehicle manufacturer, or automobile repair provider unless a specific service is expressly identified otherwise.

### CHARGING RESULTS

Charging results may vary based on the vehicle, battery condition, charging limits, battery temperature, manufacturer software, charging equipment, charging network availability, and other conditions.

Unless expressly guaranteed in writing for a specific service, Chercharge does not guarantee that every vehicle will be returned at exactly 100% battery.

### PAYMENTS

Payments for eligible physical Chercharge services and qualifying promotional purchases may be processed through an authorized third-party payment provider.

Applicable prices and mandatory charges are presented as required before the customer completes the applicable transaction.

### PRIVACY AND VEHICLE DOCUMENTATION

Where a Chercharge feature requires camera, photo, notification, or location access, the app will request applicable device permission.

## 22–31. ACCOUNT CREATION AND DELETION

Accounts may be required for core service functionality.

In-app deletion path: Profile → Settings → Privacy & Account → Delete Account

Deletion is not logout, uninstall, vehicle removal, or membership cancel.

Warn Founding Customers that deletion may terminate nontransferable promotional benefits.

Require typing DELETE and acknowledgment before final deletion. Reauthenticate reasonably without making deletion substantially harder than creation.

Backend should record deletion requests and delete or de-identify eligible data while retaining lawful payment, tax, fraud, insurance, claim, and dispute records as required.

Do not auto-reassign a $10 Founding Vehicle Rate to a new account after deletion.
